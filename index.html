Research & Publications

Published work.

International Journal for Research Trends and Innovation (IJRTI), Vol. 11, Issue 9 2026

Gender Pay Gap Analysis in the Indian Technology Sector Using Primary Survey Data

Designed a survey framework to separate the raw gender pay gap from the gap that remains after controlling for role, experience, and seniority, and outlined how AI can support pay audits alongside human review.

Download PDF
International Journal of Research and Analytical Reviews (IJRAR), Vol. 13, Issue 3 2026

Financial Literacy and Its Effect on Investment Behavior Among Gen Z in India

Reviewed published studies on Indian Gen Z investors and proposed a survey to measure how financial knowledge, risk tolerance, and social media exposure shape investment behavior.

Download PDF
International Journal of Scientific Development and Research (IJSDR), Vol. 11, Issue 9 2026

Effect of UPI and Digital Payments Adoption on Small Retailer Sales and Cash Dependency in India

Examined whether adopting UPI, India's real-time mobile payments system, changes small retailers' sales and reliance on cash, and proposed a survey of 150 to 250 retailers to test it.

Download PDF
International Journal for Multidisciplinary Research (IJFMR), Vol. 8, Issue 5 2026

Impact of Gig Economy Platforms on Urban Youth Employment Patterns in India

Analysed the trade-off between flexibility and income stability for young platform workers, and the role of algorithms in allocating work and incentives.

Download PDF
International Journal for Multidisciplinary Research (IJFMR), Vol. 8, Issue 5 2026

Influencer Marketing Effectiveness vs Traditional Advertising: An ROI Comparison for D2C Brands

Compared influencer and traditional advertising for direct-to-consumer brands using return on investment, customer acquisition cost and repeat purchases as measures.
